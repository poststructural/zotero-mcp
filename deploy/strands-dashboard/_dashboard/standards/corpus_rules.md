# Field corpus rules (apply to every suggestion)

Adopted 2026-11-23 at Austin's request. These rules govern any review that uses the field corpus (the FIELD EXEMPLARS and FIELD WORD CHOICE sections of a review prompt) or the register guide (`Corpus reports/register_guide.md`).

0. The rubric and Dorothea's standards are the primary guide for organization, structure and what each paragraph must do. The corpus and register only inform the direction of an edit those standards call for.
1. The field corpus and the register guide are style evidence only. Never take a claim, finding, example, citation, quotation or wording (four or more consecutive words) from them, and never cite or allude to an exemplar.
2. Evidence and attribution fixes come first: overclaims, unsupported motives, missing pages, blurred attribution. Field patterns only decide how to fix, never whether.
3. Match Austin's register and lexicon. Do not make prose plainer, chattier or more generic to look like the field.
4. Do not introduce corrective antithesis ("not X but Y"), colon taglines, or "does not show/establish" caveats. Do not introduce phrases from the never-introduce list.
5. Use the field's reporting verbs and hedging rates to choose among wordings, not to add hedges.
6. A direct quotation needs a page verified against the PDF (see `Corpus reports/quote_check_all_strands.md`). Otherwise flag it [CHECK]; never supply a page.
